\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{What to deploy}

\textbf{Choose an encoder by script coverage before benchmark rank.} An
Indic-targeted model is the intuitive choice for Sinhala and Tamil, and MuRIL is
the weakest neural system we measured on all three tasks, losing to character
$n$-grams on priority. The check costs a minute and no training: run the
tokenizer over the target text and count the \texttt{[UNK]} rate.

\textbf{Do not assume a native-script gain survives romanization.} On our
romanized tracks a linear model over character $n$-grams matches a multilingual
encoder, so deployments evaluated only on native-script data overestimate what
the encoder buys, in a direction that disadvantages exactly the customers who
write in romanized form.

\textbf{Spend on the ordering rule before the classifier.} Changing which
published rule consumes the posterior moves cost by a factor of three; changing
the probability model beneath it moves cost by 3\%.

\subsection{Limitations}

Each limitation below is measured rather than hedged. \textbf{The romanized
tracks are synthetic}, generated from the native tracks rather than typed by
people, so they lack the spelling variation of real romanized text
\cite{chakravarthi2020tamilmix}. Real input is harder, which makes our romanized
scores an optimistic bound and the script gap a conservative estimate.
\textbf{The Tanglish test rendering is defective} (Section~\ref{sec:corpus}) and
is excluded from every claim the paper rests on; the one finding it appears to
strengthen survives its removal. \textbf{Two label columns are model-generated},
and we report their ceilings beside every score. \textbf{The sentiment ceiling
rests on one annotator and 31 Negative tickets}, giving an interval of
$[0.667, 0.896]$ that the 90.0\%-of-ceiling claim inherits. \textbf{Intervals
are within-fit}: they resample the test set, not the training run, and one seed
repeat moved the sentiment headline by 0.0158, so differences below roughly 0.02
on sentiment should not be read as system differences. \textbf{Intent scores are
lower bounds}, still improving at the six-epoch budget. \textbf{The queue is
simulated with assumed parameters}: arrival rate, service distribution, agent
count, and service windows are assumptions, mitigated by the load sweep but the
largest threat to Section~\ref{sec:queue}. \textbf{The Tamil contrast is not
established}, and \textbf{the vocabulary mechanism is identified, not
isolated}; a character-level control at convergence \cite{clark2022canine} would
close the latter.
